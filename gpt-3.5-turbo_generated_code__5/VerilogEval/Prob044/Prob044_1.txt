```verilog
module TopModule (
  input [2:0] a,
  input [2:0] b,
  output reg [2:0] out_or_bitwise,
  output reg out_or_logical,
  output reg [5:0] out_not
);
  
  always @* begin
    out_or_bitwise = a | b;
    out_or_logical = a || b;
    out_not[5:3] = ~b;
    out_not[2:0] = ~a;
  end
  
endmodule
```

Inside the "always" block, we assign the value of the bitwise-OR of "a" and "b" to the "out_or_bitwise" output. We assign the logical-OR of "a" and "b" to the "out_or_logical" output.

To assign the inverse (NOT) of "b" in the upper half of "out_not" and the inverse of "a" in the lower half, we use bit slicing. We assign the inverse of "b" to bits [5:3] of "out_not" and the inverse of "a" to bits [2:0] of "out_not".

Note that we use "reg" datatype for the output ports because we are assigning values to them inside the "always" block.
