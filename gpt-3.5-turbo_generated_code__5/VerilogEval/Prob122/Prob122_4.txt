```verilog
module TopModule(
  input  a,

  input  d,
  output out
);

  // Intermediate signals for Karnaugh map
  wire ab_00, ab_01, ab_11, ab_10;

  // Implementing Karnaugh map
  assign ab_00 = ~(a & b & ~c & d);
  assign ab_01 = ~(a & ~b & ~c & ~d);
  assign ab_11 = ~(a & ~b & c & ~d);
  assign ab_10 = ~(~a & ~b & c & ~d);

  // Final output
  assign out = ~ab_00 & ~ab_01 & ~ab_10 & ~ab_11;

endmodule
```

This implementation uses the logical NOT (~) and logical AND (&) operators to represent the Boolean functions for the Karnaugh map. The intermediate signals ab_00, ab_01, ab_11, and ab_10 correspond to the cells in the Karnaugh map. The output signal "out" is the result of the Boolean expression derived from the Karnaugh map.

Please note that the implementation assumes that the input signals a, b, c, and d are synchronous and stable. If there might be glitches or timing issues, additional circuitry or synchronization may be required.
